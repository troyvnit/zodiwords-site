Zodiwords Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026 · Zodiwords is made by Troy Lee.

Zodiwords is an English vocabulary game for children. We collect as little as possible, we never show ads, and we never sell or share personal information for advertising.

Playing without an account

Children can play without any account. Progress (words learned, stars, streak, stickers and the chosen buddy) is stored only on the device. Nothing is sent to us.

Parent accounts (optional)

A parent can create an account to back up progress and use several devices. Children never sign in and never give us an email address.

WhatWhy
Parent's email address, and a password (stored only as a secure hash) or a sign-in from Google or AppleTo sign in and to recover the account
Each child's nickname, optional birth year and chosen zodiac buddyTo show the right profile and suggest a buddy
Each child's learning progressTo back it up and restore it on another device

With "Sign in with Apple", Apple may give us a private relay address instead of the real email.

Teacher accounts

Teachers who edit topics in Zodiwords Studio sign in with an email address and a display name. The words, sentences and pictures they publish are shown to every player and contain no personal information.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no advertising identifiers.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.
  • No chat or messaging between users.

Service providers

Account data is stored with Supabase, which hosts our database and sign-in. Google and Apple handle their sign-in when a parent chooses them. The game's voice clips are recorded in advance with Microsoft Azure AI Speech; no user data is sent to Microsoft.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep account data while the account exists. In the app's parent area (behind the parent check) a parent can remove a child, which deletes that child's profile and backup, or delete the whole account, which deletes the account and all its children and backups straight away. Progress kept only on a device is removed by deleting the app.

Children's privacy

Zodiwords is designed for children under 13 and follows the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws. Only a parent can create an account or add a child. Account features and outside links sit behind a parent check. Parents can review or delete their children's information at any time in the app, or by contacting us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell parents in the app.
