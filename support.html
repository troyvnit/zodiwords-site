Common questions

Do children need an account?

No. Children can play straight away; progress is saved on the device. A parent account is optional and backs up progress for each child, so it can move to a new phone or tablet.

How do I open the parent area?

Tap the gear on the home screen and answer the parent check. There you can sign in, add or remove a child, back up progress and delete the account.

How do I change my child's buddy?

Tap the buddy at the top of the home screen and pick another one.

Progress did not come back on a new device

Sign in with the same parent account and pick the child's profile; the latest backup is restored. Backups need an internet connection.

How do I delete our data?

See Delete an account.
