Delete a Zodiwords account

A parent can delete their Zodiwords account and all of their children's data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Zodiwords and tap the gear on the home screen.
  2. Answer the parent check, then tap Parent account & children.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This deletes the account straight away. To remove only one child, tap the pencil next to the child and choose Remove.

Without the app

Email troylee.zodiwords@gmail.com from the address you signed in with (or tell us which Google or Apple account you used), with the subject “Delete my Zodiwords account”. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • The parent account and sign-in details.
  • Every child profile (nickname, birth year, buddy) and all progress backups.

Nothing is kept afterwards. Progress stored only on a device stays there until the app is deleted.
