Zodiwords

English vocabulary game for kids · made by Troy Lee

Children pick one of 12 zodiac buddies and learn English words through pictures, a friendly voice and short games: Learn, Picture Match, Listen & Pick, Bubble Pop, Memory Match and Spell It. Topics follow the primary school curriculum (Grades 3–5). No ads, no tracking.
